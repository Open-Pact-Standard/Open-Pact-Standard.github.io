The License

OPL is a fair-source license built on the Polyform Mixed model. It draws a clear line: personal use is free, commercial use is paid, and the Maintainer controls the terms.

Not open source. OPL restricts commercial use unless the Maintainer publishes Standard Terms at a URL declared in NOTICE. That’s the trade-off, and it’s intentional. OPL protects the Maintainer’s ability to sustain their work while keeping the source open for everyone else.

Open-source software is free for everyone — including large companies that build billion-dollar products without paying the developers who made it possible. OPL changes that equation.

Under OPL, anyone can read the source, fork the project, submit patches, and use it for personal projects, education, and research. But if you want to use it commercially — as a hosted service, in a product you sell, or to provide paid services — you pay the Maintainer according to their published Standard Terms.

The Maintainer chooses everything: the price, the payment mechanism (Stripe, smart contract, bank transfer, “email me”), and the scope. OPL-Standard provides the license text and free example tools. We don’t process payments and we don’t take a cut.

The Model

Three Tiers. One License.

OPL creates a simple, three-party arrangement between the Maintainer, the source code, and the user.

🏠

Personal Use — Free

Study, modify, fork, and share. Education, research, evaluation, personal projects, and internal non-revenue tooling. No payment required. No permission needed.

Always Free
💰

Commercial Use — Paid

Hosted services, paid products, SaaS offerings, and distributing derivatives for commercial purposes. Payment goes to the Maintainer per their published Standard Terms.

Maintainer Sets Price
⚖️

Maintainer — Full Control

You set the price, the payment mechanism, and the scope. Smart contract, Stripe, GitHub Sponsors, bank wire, or “email me.” You can change terms with 90 days’ notice.

Built-In Protections

Designed for Real-World Use

OPL v1.3.1 includes provisions that other fair-source licenses don’t.

🔒

Light Copyleft

Derivatives must propagate the OPL protections — but not the code itself. You can relicense your additions under any terms, as long as the commercial-use protections remain enforced.

🤖

AI Training Addendum (Opt-In)

Want to restrict AI training on your code? OPL-AI provides canary tokens, registry fingerprinting, and attestation — all opt-in via a single line in NOTICE. Default: AI training is permitted.

⏳

Abandonment Clause

If the Maintainer is unreachable for 36 consecutive months, the Work automatically converts to Apache License 2.0 — fully permissive, fully open source. No orphaned code.

💨

90-Day Wind-Down

If the Maintainer revokes commercial use or materially changes Standard Terms, existing commercial users get a 90-day wind-down period. Previous terms continue to apply during the transition.

🌐

Functional Equivalent Protection

Can’t clean-room rewrite the Work to dodge the license. A “Functional Equivalent” is subject to the same terms for 36 months.

📋

Zero Infrastructure Required

No registry. No smart contracts. No on-chain fee collection. No framework. Copy the license, create a NOTICE file, and you’re done.

Get Started

Adopt OPL in 5 Minutes

No registry, no framework, no on-chain transactions. Five steps and you’re done.

1

Copy the License

Add LICENSE.md to your repository root.

2

Create NOTICE

Copy NOTICE.example to NOTICE. Fill in the Maintainer name, contact, jurisdiction, and Standard Terms URL.

3

Publish Standard Terms

Create a page at your declared URL with pricing, payment mechanism, and scope. Stripe, smart contract, or “email me” — your choice.

4

Add SPDX Identifier

Add SPDX-License-Identifier: OPL-1.3.1 to each source file header.

5

Update Manifest

Reference the license in pyproject.toml, Cargo.toml, or package.json.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Is OPL “open source”?
No. OPL restricts commercial use unless the Maintainer publishes Standard Terms. This disqualifies it from the Open Source Initiative’s definition. OPL is fair source — a deliberate middle ground between proprietary and open source.
What happens if the Maintainer disappears?
After 36 consecutive months of unreachability (configurable from 12–60 months in NOTICE), the Work automatically converts to Apache License 2.0 — fully permissive, fully open source. The code is never orphaned.
Can I use OPL for an open-source project?
If you want truly open source (OSI-approved), use Apache 2.0, MIT, or GPL. OPL is for Maintainers who want to keep the source public and readable while reserving the right to charge for commercial use.
What does the Maintainer have to do?
Maintain a NOTICE file with your contact and Standard Terms URL. Respond to licensing inquiries within 60 days. You choose the pricing, the payment mechanism, and the scope.
Does OPL restrict AI training?
By default, no. The OPL-AI addendum is opt-in. If the Maintainer declares OPL-AI: opted in. in NOTICE, AI training is restricted. The addendum includes canary tokens, registry fingerprinting, and attestation mechanisms.
Who enforces the license?
The Maintainer, through standard contract enforcement. OPL is a private license — a contract between the Maintainer and the user. Disputes are resolved under the jurisdiction declared in NOTICE.
